About Me

I am an Assistant Professor at the College of Forestry, Guangxi University. My research focuses on forest parameter mapping and ecological remote sensing, leveraging spaceborne LiDAR (GEDI, ICESat-2), multispectral/SAR satellite imagery (Sentinel-1/2, Landsat), and deep learning / machine learning methods to enable large-scale, high spatiotemporal resolution monitoring of forest structural parameters (canopy height, forest type, biomass, etc.).

My current core project is annual canopy height mapping in Guangxi (2019–2026), addressing accuracy challenges in karst complex terrain and capturing inter-annual signals of plantation and natural forest growth, disturbance, and recovery. Target journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (RSE).

Research interests span: spatiotemporal deep learning (3D CNN / ConvLSTM / Temporal Attention), multi-source remote sensing data fusion, LiDAR point cloud processing, forest carbon stock estimation, and plantation vs. natural forest remote sensing monitoring.

Research Interests

🌲

Forest Canopy Height Mapping

Spaceborne LiDAR (GEDI / ICESat-2) footprints as training labels combined with Sentinel multispectral/SAR imagery and deep learning for 10 m resolution continuous canopy height retrieval.

📈

Temporal Dynamics Monitoring

From static mapping to spatiotemporal modeling using Sentinel-2 time-series features (NDVI trajectories, phenological metrics) to capture inter-annual forest growth, disturbance, and recovery.

🏔️

LiDAR Accuracy in Complex Terrain

Evaluating GEDI and ICESat-2 canopy height retrieval accuracy in karst peak-forest/depression landforms, optimizing slope filtering and terrain correction strategies.

🌍

Carbon Stock Estimation

Converting canopy height products to aboveground biomass (AGB) via random forest and other models to support regional carbon budget assessment and forest management decisions.

🧠

Spatiotemporal Deep Learning

Exploring 3D CNN, ConvLSTM, Temporal Attention architectures for remote sensing time-series modeling to improve inter-annual change detection capability.

🛰️

Multi-Source Remote Sensing Fusion

Complementary use of GEDI (high accuracy, ended 2023) and ICESat-2 (ongoing) to build longer temporal coverage for canopy height monitoring.
